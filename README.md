# ResumeCraft for Students 🎓
> An interactive, decluttered, question-guided resume building web application designed specifically for college students, campus placement drives, and entry-level applicants.

---

## 🌟 Key Updates & Features

### 1. 🧼 Decluttered, Clean & Easy-to-Follow Flow
- **Minimalist Layout**: Reduced visual clutter by streamlining the ATS widget into a compact, elegant status bar, giving the Question Wizard the full spotlight.
- **Start Fresh (No Prefill)**: Begins with a clean, blank slate. Input fields are empty, allowing students to type their own real details from scratch. Subtle placeholders guide you with helpful formatting hints.
- **Easy-to-Follow Wizard**: One focused question at a time with large inputs, friendly student pro-tips, and clear `Continue to Next Step →` controls.

### 2. 📷 Photo Option (With or Without Photo)
- **1-Click Photo Toggle**: Turn your profile photo on or off anytime with a single toggle switch:
  - Turn **ON** for Indian campus placement drives, European CVs, or creative portfolios.
  - Turn **OFF** for standard ATS text-only screening.
- **Instant Photo Uploader**: Upload your formal headshot directly from your device (stored locally in state and browser storage).

- **Expanded Templates**:
  - **Indian Campus Placement Standard**: Top-right photo box, placement dividers, structured academic qualifications.
  - **Tech Modern (Clean ATS)**: Horizontal accent rules, optimized for software and data roles.
  - **Executive Ivy / Academic**: Serif typography (Merriweather), traditional academic formatting.
  - **Clean Minimalist**: High contrast, Swiss typographical design.
  - **Modern Split Column**: Dual-column layout with sidebar photo and contact info.

### 4. 🇮🇳 Top Indian Companies & Campus Recruiters Match
- **Real-Time Compatibility Engine**: Matches your resume keywords, projects, and CGPA against hiring rubrics for:
  - **Indian Product & Unicorns**: Flipkart, Zoho Corporation, Razorpay / CRED, Swiggy / Zomato.
  - **Indian IT & Campus Placement**: TCS (Digital & Ninja), Infosys (Specialist Programmer & Systems Engineer).
  - **Global Tech & Finance**: Google, Microsoft, Amazon / AWS, Goldman Sachs.
- **CGPA out of 10**: Built for the 10-point CGPA grading system (e.g. `8.75 / 10 CGPA`). Scores $\ge 8.0/10$ boost eligibility for top-tier hiring tracks.

### 5. 💾 Rock-Solid Local Storage Auto-Save
- **Never Lose Progress**: Every keystroke is saved immediately to your browser's local storage.
- **Live Status Indicator**: A visible `✓ Auto-saved` pill in the header confirms your work is safely preserved. Even if you close the tab or turn off your computer, your data will be right there when you reopen the site!
